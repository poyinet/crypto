莱昂·普莱费尔勋爵（Lyon Playfair）

> 人物志 · 🃏 · 科学家 · Playfair 密码得名者 · 1818–1898 · 英国

简介

中文：这里要先澄清一个「挂名」：Playfair 密码其实不是他发明的，而是 1854 年由查尔斯·惠斯通（电话的发明者）设计；普莱费尔只是把它写成书、普及开来，于是后人一直叫「Playfair 密码」。这个双表替换算法用一张 5×5 字母表把明文两两成对加密，曾是 19 世纪英军的标准装备，一战初期也还在用。它不是最安全的，却是「把密码写成可操作的算法表」这件事的典范。

English: First a correction to the name: the Playfair cipher was not his invention but was designed in 1854 by Charles Wheatstone (the telephone's inventor); Playfair merely published it and made it famous, so posterity has called it the "Playfair cipher". This double-square substitution encrypts plaintext in letter pairs using a 5x5 alphabet table; it was the British army's standard kit in the 19th century and was still in early use in WWI. It was not the most secure, but it was the model of "writing a cipher as an operable algorithm table".

一句话

中文：电话之父惠斯通设计了 Playfair 密码，却一辈子没被记上一笔——「挂名」的教科书案例。

English: Telephone pioneer Wheatstone designed the Playfair cipher yet never got credit for a day — the textbook case of a "borrowed name".
