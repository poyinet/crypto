马尔科姆·威廉森

[人物] 马尔科姆·威廉森 · e12-cold-war-info-theory · L3

时代冷战与信息论转折难度专家

马尔科姆·威廉森（Malcolm Williamson）

> 人物志 · 🔢 · 英国密码学家 · GCHQ 密钥交换先驱 · 1950–2013 · 英国

简介

中文：GCHQ 的数学家。1974 年他和 Cocks、Davies 同组，独立推导出了公钥密码的密钥交换原理——两人共享秘密、公钥可公开分发——早于 Diffie-Hellman 的公开论文。成果同样被保密，直到 1996 年才解密，证明英国在 70 年代初期就已想通公钥，只是没有发表。他是「公钥三杰」之一，也是公钥史上「独立到达」的典型。

English: A GCHQ mathematician. In 1974, working with Cocks and Davies, he independently derived the key-exchange principle of public-key crypto — two parties sharing a secret, public keys freely distributed — before Diffie-Hellman's public paper. The result stayed classified until a 1996 declassification, proving Britain had already worked out public-key in the early 70s, just never published. He is one of the "public-key trio" and the textbook case of "independent arrival" in public-key history.

一句话

中文：他和 Cocks、Davies 都早于 RSA 想通了公钥，却因保密制度集体失声二十年。

English: He, Cocks, and Davies had all worked out public-key before RSA, yet went silent for twenty years under classification.
