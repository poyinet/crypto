亚当·贝克

[人物] 亚当·贝克 · e13-public-key · L3

时代公钥革命难度专家

亚当·贝克（Adam Back）

> 人物志 · ⛓️ · 密码朋克 · Hashcash 发明者 · 1970– · 英国/马耳他

简介

中文：密码朋克（cypherpunk）运动的元老之一，也是区块链研究的核心推手。1990 年代他在那个名闻天下的密码朋克邮件列表上发言——1993 年 Eric Hughes 写下「密码朋克宣言」（我们要用密码学作武器，把隐私还给普通人），正是那个列表孕育的思潮。PGP 的普及、对政府监控的抵抗，都从这十几年网络辩论里长出来。后来他作为 Blockstream 联合创始人，持续推动比特币的侧链与协议扩展研究，把「用密码学重新设计金融基础设施」这条线一直走到今天。

English: A senior figure in the cypherpunk movement and a core driver of blockchain research. In the 1990s he was active on the legendary cypherpunk mailing list — the 1993 "Cypherpunk's Manifesto" by Eric Hughes ("we will use cryptography as a weapon to give privacy back to ordinary people") was born from exactly that list's debate. The spread of PGP, the resistance to government surveillance, all grew from those years of online argument. Later, as a Blockstream co-founder, he keeps pushing Bitcoin's sidechains and protocol-extension research, carrying the line of "redesigning financial infrastructure with cryptography" to today.

一句话

中文：密码朋克宣言（1993）那句「我们要用密码学作武器」，出自他与同辈活跃的同一批邮件列表。

English: The 1993 Cypherpunk Manifesto line "we will use cryptography as a weapon" comes from the very mailing list he and his peers were active on.
