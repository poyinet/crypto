莱昂·巴蒂斯塔·阿尔贝蒂

[人物] 莱昂·巴蒂斯塔·阿尔贝蒂 · e06-renaissance · L3

时代文艺复兴与宗教改革难度专家

莱昂·巴蒂斯塔·阿尔贝蒂（Leon Battista Alberti）

> 人物志 · 💿 · 文艺复兴通才 · 密码盘发明者 · 1404–1472 · 意大利

简介

中文：建筑师、画家、音乐家、数学家——典型的文艺复兴全才。1467 年他秘密发明了「阿尔贝蒂方格」：一张圆盘上套两层不同的字母表，每加密一个字母就把圆盘转一格，等于「每个字母换一张字母表」。这张盘比维吉尼亚早了一百余年，是多表替换的发端。更精彩的是后面的故事：这套密码他藏了近三十年，直到向教皇庇护二世坦白，教皇震怒、把他关进监狱、威胁绞刑。眼看要死，他当众道出圆盘的秘密自保，教皇赦了他，让他继续留下当教廷建筑师。一个把密码当秘密攥了三十年、最后靠「说破」换来命的人——加密史里最戏剧化的一笔。

English: Architect, painter, musician and mathematician — the quintessential Renaissance man. In 1467 he secretly invented the "Alberti square": a disk with two different alphabets, turned one letter at a time so each letter used a new alphabet table. It predates Vigenère by over a century and is the birth of polyalphabetic substitution. The more remarkable story is what followed: he kept this cipher a secret for nearly thirty years, then confessed to Pope Pius II, who was furious, had him imprisoned, and threatened to hang him. Facing death, Alberti publicly revealed the disk's secret to save himself; the pope spared his life and kept him on as court architect. A man who held a cipher like a secret for thirty years and then "spoke" his way out of a noose — the most dramatic episode in the history of encryption.

一句话

中文：他靠向教皇坦白圆盘的秘密才免于一死——加密史上唯一一次「说破密码保命」的真实案例。

English: He only escaped death by revealing the disk's secret to the pope — the only real case in encryption history of "talking one out of a noose."
