詹姆斯·埃利斯

[人物] 詹姆斯·埃利斯 · e13-public-key · L3

时代公钥革命难度专家

詹姆斯·埃利斯（James Ellis）

> 人物志 · 💡 · 「非秘密加密」的提出者 · 公钥思想的隐秘先驱 · 1924–1997 · 英国

简介

中文：詹姆斯·埃利斯是英国 GCHQ 的密码学家。1969 年，他提出「非秘密加密」：加密与解密使用不同密钥，加密密钥可以公开，双方无需事先秘密交换——这正是公钥密码学的核心思想。他当时无法证明其可行性，便把问题交给同事；科克斯与威廉森据此先后发明了与 RSA、迪菲-赫尔曼等价的方案。这批成果因保密尘封近三十年，1997 年才解密公开。

English: James Ellis was a British cryptographer at GCHQ. In 1969 he formulated the concept of "non-secret encryption": if encryption and decryption use different keys, the encryption key can be made public and two parties need no prior secret exchange — the essential idea of public-key cryptography. He could not prove it was possible, so he handed the problem to colleagues; Cocks and Williamson turned it into working schemes years ahead of the open literature. Classified for nearly three decades, Ellis's work was declassified only in 1997.

一句话

中文：冷知识：埃利斯 1987 年写下的内部回顾《非秘密加密的历史》如今是公钥密码隐秘史的第一手档案——解密后，学界称他为「公钥密码学被遗忘的祖父」；他 1997 年去世，几乎没等到这份迟到 28 年的承认。

English: Fun fact: Ellis's 1987 internal memoir, "The History of Non-Secret Encryption," is now the primary document of public-key cryptography's secret chapter — after declassification he was called "the forgotten grandfather of public-key cryptography." He died in 1997, just before the belated recognition arrived.

名言

中文：我提出了一扇门，却打不开它——但门的存在本身，就是答案的一半。（意译，编者拟）

English: I pointed at a door I could not open — but knowing the door exists was half the answer. (paraphrased)
