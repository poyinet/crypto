梅雷迪思·加德纳

[人物] 梅雷迪思·加德纳 · e12-cold-war-info-theory · L3

时代冷战与信息论转折难度专家

梅雷迪思·加德纳（Meredith Gardner）

> 人物志 · 🕵️ · 语言学家 · VENONA 破译核心 · 1912–2002 · 美国

简介

中文：密歇根大学语文学家，1940 年代被陆军信号情报局征召。他从二战期间未销毁的苏方电报存档中还原出一次性密码本重用，开创 VENONA 计划，识别出原子间谍网与数百名潜伏特工。

English: A University of Michigan philologist drafted into the Army's Signal Intelligence Service in the 1940s, he exploited reused Soviet one-time pads in archived wartime cables to found the VENONA program — exposing an atomic espionage ring and hundreds of covered agents.

一句话

中文：他不懂密码学却懂古法语与德语文学——1946 年仅凭苏联贸易电报里的重复数字残迹重建了苏联间谍体制的骨架，让罗森堡案浮出水面。同事说他「用读诗的方法读密文」。

English: A scholar of Old French and German literature who knew no cryptology, he rebuilt the skeleton of Soviet espionage in 1946 from repeated number-groups in trade traffic — the Rosenberg case surfaced from his reading. Colleagues said he "read ciphertext like poetry."

名言

中文：重复，是一切秘密的坟墓。

English: Repetition is the grave of every secret.
