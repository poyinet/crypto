杰里·罗伯茨

[人物] 杰里·罗伯茨 · e11-ww2 · L3

时代第二次世界大战难度专家

杰里·罗伯茨（Jerry Roberts）

> 人物志 · 🗂️ · 英国密码学家 · Testery 首席之一 · 1920–2014 · 英国

简介

中文：Colossus 团队的数学家。洛伦兹（Lorenz）电传密码的破译，靠的不是蛮力而是他这类人设计的「差动器」——一种按统计规律逐位消减候选密钥的机电筛选机。Roberts 参与了这套统计引擎的推导，是「把数学破译变成机器」的关键一环。后来他还参与 Colossus 的运作。那个年代，破译一台机器，靠的是一屋子这样的人把统计公式变成继电器。

English: A mathematician on the Colossus team. Breaking the Lorenz teleprinter cipher relied not on brute force but on the "differencers" designed by people like him — an electromechanical sieve that eliminated candidate keys bit by bit by statistical law. Roberts helped derive the statistical engine behind it, a key link in "turning mathematical cryptanalysis into a machine". He later took part in operating Colossus. In that era, breaking one machine depended on a room full of such people turning statistics into relays.

一句话

中文：差动器（differencing machine）是 Colossus 之前、用继电器搭出来的统计破译机，Roberts 是设计者之一。

English: The differencing machine — a statistical codebreaker built from relays before Colossus — was one he helped design.
