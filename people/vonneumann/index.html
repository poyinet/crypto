时代冷战与信息论转折难度专家

约翰·冯·诺依曼（John von Neumann）

> 人物志 · 🧠 · 数学家 · 现代计算机架构之父 · 1903–1957 · 匈牙利/美国

简介

中文：横跨物理、数学、经济学的天才。曼哈顿计划里他参与设计原子弹，同时是「存储程序计算机」概念的提出者——今天的冯·诺依曼架构（指令存内存、按序取指）就是他定的。密码学上，他在流密码领域提出过基于伪随机序列的方案，是早期把「随机性」当密码基石的人之一。一个能把核武器、计算机、博弈论全装进同一套数学的人，在密码学里留下的，是「随机序列加密」这条最早的路。

English: A genius spanning physics, mathematics, and economics. On the Manhattan Project he helped design the atomic bomb while proposing the "stored-program computer" concept — the von Neumann architecture of today (instructions in memory, fetched in order) is his. In cryptography he proposed schemes based on pseudorandom sequences, among the earliest to treat "randomness" as the foundation of secrecy. One man who could fit nuclear weapons, computers, and game theory into the same mathematics; what he left cryptography is the earliest path of "random-sequence encryption".

一句话

中文：存储程序计算机的「冯·诺依曼架构」以他命名——你正在用的一台机器，就按他的设计跑。

English: The "von Neumann architecture" of stored-program computers is named after him — the very machine you are reading this on runs to his design.
