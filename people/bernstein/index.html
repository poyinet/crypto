丹尼尔·伯恩斯坦

[人物] 丹尼尔·伯恩斯坦 · e13-public-key · L3

时代公钥革命难度专家

丹尼尔·伯恩斯坦（Daniel J. Bernstein）

> 人物志 · 🦉 · 密码学家 · 曲线 25519 与 ChaCha20 之父 · 1971– · 美国

简介

中文：美国密码学家、密码实现安全的旗手。他提出并推广了 EdDSA/Ed25519（Edwards 曲线上的数字签名）、Poly1305 认证标签、SIPHASH 与 ChaCha20——如今互联网上跑着的高速密码几乎都出自他手。但他最有名的贡献是「密码实现必须防侧信道」：他反复警告算法写得再对，C 代码里一个分支、一次内存访问都可能泄密，并亲自写出常数时间的实现。他主持的「密码实现安全」项目，把「实现即安全」变成了一门手艺。

English: An American cryptographer and the standard-bearer for secure crypto implementation. He proposed and promoted EdDSA/Ed25519 (digital signatures over Edwards curves), Poly1305 authentication tags, SIPHASH, and ChaCha20 — the fast ciphers running on the internet today are almost all his. But his most famous contribution is "crypto implementations must resist side channels": he repeatedly warned that however right the algorithm, one branch or one memory access in the C code can leak a secret, and wrote constant-time implementations himself. His "secure crypto implementation" project turned "the implementation is the security" into a craft.

一句话

中文：Ed25519 的「Ed」取自他的名字，曲线由他设计、签名算法与 Provencher 等共同完成。

English: The "Ed" in Ed25519 comes from his name — the curve is his, the signature scheme co-completed with Provencher and others.
