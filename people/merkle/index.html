拉尔夫·默克尔（Ralph Merkle）

> 人物志 · 🌳 · 公钥密码的先驱 · Merkle 树发明人 · 1952– · 美国

简介

中文：拉尔夫·默克尔是美国计算机科学家。1974 年，还是加州大学伯克利分校本科生的他提出「默克尔谜题」：双方通过公开信道交换海量谜题即可协商出共享密钥——这是史上第一个公钥密码构想，与迪菲-赫尔曼并列公钥密码学的开创者。他还发明了哈希树（Merkle 树），如今是区块链与分布式系统的核心数据结构。

English: Ralph Merkle is an American computer scientist. In 1974, while still an undergraduate at Berkeley, he proposed "Merkle's puzzles": two parties exchange a large number of puzzles over a public channel and thereby agree on a shared secret — the first public-key scheme ever conceived, ranking him alongside Diffie and Hellman as a founder of public-key cryptography. He also invented the hash tree, universally known today as the Merkle tree, which underpins blockchains and countless distributed systems.

一句话

中文：冷知识：默克尔的「谜题」论文 1975 年曾被《美国计算机学会通讯》以「想法太过超前」退稿，直到 1978 年才发表——此时迪菲-赫尔曼的论文早已问世，公钥密码的功劳簿上，他只能与别人并列署名。

English: Fun fact: Merkle's puzzles paper was rejected by the Communications of the ACM in 1975 as "too far out" and only appeared in 1978 — by then Diffie–Hellman had already been published, so his place in history became a shared one.

名言

中文：在公开的世界里，也可以藏得住只有两个人知道的秘密。

English: Even in a public world, two people can share a secret no one else knows.
