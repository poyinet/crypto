时代第一次世界大战难度专家

查尔斯·惠斯通（Charles Wheatstone）

> 人物志 · 🌉 · 物理学家与发明家 · Playfair 密码的创造者 · 1802–1875 · 英国

简介

中文：惠斯通是维多利亚时代的科学全才：与库克合作发明实用电报，推广了以他命名的电桥，还痴迷于用机器藏信。1854 年他发明 Playfair 密码——把明文按字母两两分组，用 5×5 方阵整体加密，单字母的频率统计从此失效。它由好友莱昂·普莱费尔引荐给军方并冠名，后被英军用于布尔战争与一战，成为「双字母加密」的经典范本。

English: Wheatstone was a Victorian polymath: with Cooke he built the first practical electric telegraph, popularised the bridge that bears his name, and loved mechanical secrecy. In 1854 he devised the Playfair cipher — plaintext is split into letter pairs and enciphered as a whole through a 5×5 square, so single-letter frequency analysis collapses. His friend Lord Lyon Playfair pitched it to the War Office and lent it his name; the British Army later used it in the Boer War and WWI, making it the classic example of digraph encryption.

一句话

中文：冷知识：他名下最出名的两样东西其实都不「属于」他——Playfair 密码以推广者普莱费尔命名，惠斯通电桥的真正发明者是克里斯蒂；倒是那台用偏振光传送密文的「惠斯通密码机」，才是他亲手造的原汁原味发明。

English: Fun fact: the two most famous things in his name weren't really his — the Playfair cipher honours its promoter Lord Playfair, and the Wheatstone bridge was invented by Samuel Hunter Christie. His genuinely own invention was the Wheatstone cryptograph, a machine that sent secret messages by polarised light.

名言

中文：「让字母两两起舞，单字母的频率统计就失去了准头。」（概括 Playfair 密码的设计思想）

English: "Make the letters dance in pairs, and single-letter frequency analysis loses its aim." (a paraphrase of the Playfair design idea)
