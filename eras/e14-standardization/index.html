标准化时代（Age of Standardization）

> 1977–2001 · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：算法从论文走进标准，是这一代的主题。1977 年美国把 IBM 的 Lucifer 改进后定为 DES（数据加密标准，56 位密钥），它是第一个「全民共用」的对称算法；但 DES 的密钥太短，20 世纪末已被暴力破解淘汰。于是 NIST 公开征集新一代算法，1997 年起评，2001 年宣布 Rijndael（荷兰人 Daemen 与 Rijmen）胜出，成为 AES——128 位分组、128/192/256 位密钥、10/12/14 轮，至今仍是事实标准。同代还有 SHA 系列（SHA-1 已被攻破、SHA-256 现役）成为哈希标准。这一代确立「开放、公开评审」的算法选择方式，算法安全从此靠全世界的破解尝试来检验。

English: Algorithms moving from paper into standards is this generation’s theme. In 1977 the US adopted IBM’s Lucifer (improved) as DES (Data Encryption Standard, 56-bit key) — the first "everyone uses the same one" symmetric algorithm; but DES’s key was too short and was brute-forced out by the end of the century. So NIST opened a public search for the next generation, ran the evaluation from 1997, and in 2001 declared Rijndael (Dutchmen Daemen and Rijmen) the winner, becoming AES — 128-bit blocks, 128/192/256-bit keys, 10/12/14 rounds, still the de facto standard today. The SHA family (SHA-1 broken, SHA-256 current) became the hash standard. This generation established "open, publicly reviewed" algorithm selection; algorithm security is now tested by the whole world’s attempts to break it.
