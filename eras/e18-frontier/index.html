前沿（The Frontier）

> 今 / Today · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：当下的三条前沿线：完全同态加密（FHE）——在密文上直接计算，解密前不出结果；后量子迁移——NIST 2024 标准（ML-KEM/ML-DSA/SLH-DSA）正式上岗，混合方案成为过渡默认；AI 与密码分析——大模型与对抗机器学习开始参与密码分析与侧信道推断。量子安全网络、隐私计算、AI 安全是未来十年的主战场。

English: Three frontier lines today: fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) — compute on ciphertext without decrypting; post-quantum migration — NIST’s 2024 standards (ML-KEM/ML-DSA/SLH-DSA) have entered service and hybrid schemes are the transitional default; AI and cryptanalysis — LLMs and adversarial ML are beginning to participate in cryptanalysis and side-channel inference. Quantum-safe networks, privacy computing, and AI security are the next decade’s main battlegrounds.
