破译者时代（Age of Codebreaking）

> 1789–1914 · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：电报的到来把密码学从外交柜子里拽出来，变成国家机器的核心。1844 年摩斯发明电码，电报通信瞬间成为战争与外交的命脉——而电报加密就是密码学的新战场。这一代是「破译」的黄金期：法国人巴比宁（Painvin，1893 年独立破译维吉尼亚，被军方雪藏到 1929 才发表）、美国人弗里德曼、德军 40 号房，把频率分析和统计方法练成一套工业化的破译流程。维吉尼亚密码在 19 世纪被巴贝奇（1854）和卡西斯基（Kasiski 1863，用重复字母的间隔找周期）相继打穿，「不可解」的头号名号终于易主。这一代确立了「密码分析是一门学科」的事实，为 20 世纪机器时代的到来铺好了路。

English: The telegraph dragged cryptography out of the diplomatic cabinet and into the heart of the state machine. When Morse invented the code in 1844, telegraph traffic became the lifeline of war and diplomacy overnight — and telegraph encryption became cryptography’s new battlefield. This generation was the golden age of breaking: the Frenchman Painvin (who independently broke Vigenère in 1893 but was suppressed by the army until 1929), the American Friedman, and the German Room 40 turned frequency analysis and statistics into an industrialized breaking pipeline. The Vigenère cipher was cracked in the 19th century by Babbage (1854) and Kasiski (1863, using repeated-letter intervals to find the period) in turn, and the "indecipherable" title finally changed hands. This generation established that "cryptanalysis is a discipline," paving the way for the machine age of the 20th century.
