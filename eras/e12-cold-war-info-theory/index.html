冷战与信息论转折（Cold War & Information Theory）

> 1945–1975 · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：冷战让密码学第一次有了理论根基。1949 年香农发表《通信的数学理论》，用信息论的形式化给「完美保密」下了定义——他证明 Vernam 的一次性密码（OTP）是唯一能达到完美保密的密码，也给出了熵、混淆、扩散这些至今仍是密码学基石的概念。同一时期，美苏的 VENONA 工程（美方从 1940 年代开始破译苏联密钥）暴露了 OTP 复用（同一条密钥流用两次）的致命漏洞——数学上的「唯一完美」被人为的「省事」击穿。这一代还见证了 NSA 和 GCHQ 的成立，密码学从战场工具变成情报工业的支柱。

English: The Cold War gave cryptography its first theoretical foundation. In 1949 Shannon published A Mathematical Theory of Communication, giving "perfect secrecy" a formal definition in information theory — he proved Vernam’s one-time pad (OTP) is the only cipher that can reach perfect secrecy, and introduced entropy, confusion, and diffusion, concepts that remain cryptography’s bedrock. In the same period, the US-Soviet VENONA project (America breaking Soviet keys from the 1940s) exposed the fatal flaw of OTP reuse (using the same keystream twice) — the mathematical "unique perfection" was pierced by human "convenience." This generation also saw the founding of NSA and GCHQ, cryptography moving from a battlefield tool to the pillar of the intelligence industry.
