中国平行线（China's Parallel Line）

> 横切 / Cross-cutting · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：中国密码学从先秦阴符（兵符验信）到《六韬》阴书（拆信防截），到明将戚继光反切码，是一条与西方并行的密写传统。1930 年龙冈战斗缴获的半部电台，发展为曾希圣领导的红军无线电侦察——与同时代任何信号情报部队并驾齐驱。当代中国以 SM2/SM3/SM4/ZUC 四件套与《密码法》（2020）从「用标准」走向「立标准」，并率先把量子密钥分发投入运营。

English: Chinese cryptography runs parallel to the West, from the pre-Qin yinfu (tally authentication) and the Liu Tao’s yinshu (split-letter anti-intercept) to Qi Jiguang’s fanqie code — a distinct secrecy tradition. The "half radio" captured at Longgang (1930) grew into Zeng Xisheng’s Red Army signals intelligence, matching any contemporary SIGINT force. Modern China moved from "using standards" to "making standards" via the SM2/SM3/SM4/ZUC family and the Cryptography Law (2020), and first to operationalize quantum key distribution.
