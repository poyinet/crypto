拜占庭与早期伊斯兰（Byzantine & Early Islamic）

> 476–800 · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：西罗马 476 年陷落，但东罗马（拜占庭）把罗马的密写传统一路带进中世纪——行政与军情仍依赖移位与替换。真正的转折发生在伊斯兰世界：阿拉伯语从 7 世纪起成为科学语言，密码学随之进入「翻译—记录」阶段。阿拉伯学者不仅保存了希腊密码思想，更开始用阿拉伯文字母的频率特性研究密码——为下一个时代的黄金时代埋下种子。

English: After the fall of the Western Roman Empire in 476, the Eastern (Byzantine) Empire carried Roman cipher practice into the medieval era — administration and military traffic still relied on shift and substitution. The real pivot came in the Islamic world: Arabic became the language of science from the 7th century, and cryptography entered a "translation-and-recording" phase. Arab scholars preserved Greek cipher ideas and began analyzing ciphers using the letter-frequency properties of the Arabic script — planting the seed for the coming Golden Age.
