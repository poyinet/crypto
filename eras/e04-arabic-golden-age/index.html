阿拉伯黄金时代（Islamic Golden Age）

> 800–1258 · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：密码学从「手艺」变成「学问」，是在阿拉伯世界。巴格达的智慧宫（House of Wisdom）里，阿拉伯学者把希腊的密写文献译成阿拉伯文，并开始用数学研究它。最重要的转折是频率分析：al-Kindi（约 850 年）在《论破译密信》里指出，每种语言的字母出现频率是固定的——阿拉伯语「ا」最多，就像英文「e」最多——数一数密文里哪个符号最频繁，替换密码就现形了。这一招对单表替换（凯撒、Atbash 那一族）是致命的，因为它们的频率没被抹平。频率分析是密码分析学的起点，它把「破译」从猜测升级成统计，奠定了此后一千年的破译范式。

English: Cryptography became a discipline rather than a craft in the Arab world. In Baghdad’s House of Wisdom, Arab scholars translated Greek cipher texts into Arabic and began to study them mathematically. The decisive turn was frequency analysis: al-Kindi (c. 850) wrote in On Deciphering Cryptographic Messages that every language has fixed letter frequencies — Arabic «ا» is most common, as «e» is in English — so counting the most frequent symbol in a ciphertext exposes a substitution cipher. This trick was fatal to monoalphabetic substitution (the Caesar/Atbash family), because their frequencies were left untouched. Frequency analysis was the beginning of cryptanalysis, elevating "breaking" from guesswork to statistics, and setting the paradigm for the next thousand years.
