量子转折（Quantum Turn）

> 1984–2019 · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：量子力学从物理课走进了密码学，而且是双向的。1984 年 Bennett 和 Brassard 提出 BB84 协议，利用量子不可克隆定理做密钥分发（QKD）——窃听即扰动、扰动即被发现，安全性由物理定律保证而非计算困难。同一时期，Shor 算法（1994）证明量子计算机能多项式时间分解大数、解离散对数——这意味着今天的 RSA、ECC 在足够大的量子机面前全部失守。Grover 算法给出量子搜索的平方加速，对称密码的密钥长度因此要加倍。这一代是「量子既是武器也是盾牌」的开端：量子既能破掉公钥，也能用物理原理造出无法被偷听的新通信。

English: Quantum mechanics walked into cryptography from the physics classroom — in both directions. In 1984 Bennett and Brassard proposed BB84, using the no-cloning theorem for quantum key distribution (QKD) — eavesdropping perturbs the state, and perturbation is detection, so security rests on physical law rather than computational hardness. In the same period, Shor’s algorithm (1994) showed a quantum computer can factor integers and solve discrete logs in polynomial time — meaning today’s RSA and ECC all fall before a sufficiently large quantum machine. Grover’s algorithm gives quadratic speedup for quantum search, so symmetric keys must double in length. This generation is the start of "quantum as both sword and shield": quantum can break public-key, yet physical law can build new communication that cannot be eavesdropped.
