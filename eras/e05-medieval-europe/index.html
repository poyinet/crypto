中世纪欧洲（Medieval Europe）

> 1258–1450 · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：1258 年蒙古 sack 巴格达，阿拉伯黄金时代落幕，密码学重心移回欧洲。中世纪欧洲的密写服务于秘密外交：教廷、各国王室各有一套「名册」（nomenclator）与代号体系，机密信件以替换或密语写成。这段时期的密码是国家机密而非科学，直到文艺复兴，数学才重新介入，密码学才从手艺变成学问。

English: After the 1258 sack of Baghdad, the Arab Golden Age ended and cryptography’s center of gravity shifted back to Europe. Medieval European secrecy served secret diplomacy: the papacy and royal courts each kept a "nomenclator" and code system, with confidential letters written in substitution or code. Cipher practice was a state secret, not a science — until the Renaissance, when mathematics re-entered and cryptography became a field of study again.
