战间期（Interwar Period）

> 1918–1939 · 轴1 编年叙事

中文：两次大战之间，转子机从德军的 Enigma 升级为国家安全工具，而密码学第一次成为一门工业。波兰密码局（Biuro Szyfrów）的雷耶夫斯基、罗życki、齐加利斯克在 1932–39 年间连续破解德军 Enigma——这是「人类 vs 机器」的第一次胜利。德国则把 Enigma 加到 4 转子、加插线板，为二战的大对决上了膛。这段时期确立了「破译即工业」的新范式。

English: Between the wars, rotor machines rose from the German Enigma to a national-security tool, and cryptography became an industry for the first time. The Polish Cipher Bureau’s Rejewski, Różycki, and Zygalski broke the Wehrmacht Enigma in 1932–39 — the first human victory over a machine. Germany, in turn, hardened the Enigma to 4 rotors and a plugboard, loading the great WW2 duel. This period established the "codebreaking as industry" paradigm.
