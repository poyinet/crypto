洛伦兹 SZ40/42（Tunny）（Lorenz SZ40/42 (Tunny)）

> 机器图鉴 · 1941–1945 · 德国 / 1941–1945 · Germany · 关联游戏：lorenz

概述

中文：十二轮电传密码机：5 比特博多码流上做异或，比 Enigma 更「现代」。

English: Twelve wheels on a 5-bit Baudot teleprinter stream, doing XOR — more modern in concept than Enigma.

历史

中文：德国最高层用它传输战略级电报。破译它需要「差分统计」（Δ 运算）找规律，而计算量催生了 Colossus——世界第一台可编程电子计算机，每秒处理 5,000 字符。图灵曾赴布莱切利园指导 Tunny 破译，这是电子计算机的隐秘摇篮。

English: Germany's highest command used it for strategic traffic. Breaking it demanded differencing (Δ) to expose structure, and the compute load gave birth to Colossus — the first programmable electronic computer, 5,000 chars per second. Turing visited Bletchley to advise on Tunny; this was computing's secret cradle.
