字验军符册（Ziyan Field Manual）

> 密件册 · 1044 · 北宋 · 性质：据史料复原 / Reconstructed · 关联游戏：substitution

说明

中文：《武经总要》前集所载「字验」：选一首四十字的五言诗为底本，以主将临时分发的钥字定诗中一字，其位次对应「请弓」「请粮」「被贼围」等四十项军情之一。钥字逐日更换，码本随之而换——千年前的「日密钥」实践。以下为依原制重构的一页。

English: The "ziyan" method in the Wujing Zongyao (1044): choose a forty-character poem as the base; a key character issued daily by the general selects one position, which maps to one of forty pre-agreed tactical reports such as "request arrows" or "besieged". Change the key character and the whole encoding changes — a thousand-year-old daily-key practice. Below, one page reconstructed from the original system.

全文 / 摘录

中文：字验之法（依《武经总要》重构）：以「国破山河在，城春草木深……」一首四十字、字不重复的五言诗为底，临阵之际主将密定一钥字。得诗中第七字，则验为「请增兵」；第十九字，「粮尽」；第三十三字，「贼夜袭」——凡四十事，事事有位。诗在人前，码在人心：不知当日钥字者，纵览全诗亦无所获。

English: The ziyan method (reconstructed from the Wujing Zongyao): forty five-character regulated verses serve as the base — "The nation shattered, mountains and rivers remain…" On campaign the general secretly fixes one key character. Draw the seventh character of a verse and it reads "request reinforcements"; the nineteenth, "grain exhausted"; the thirty-third, "night raid coming" — forty reports, each with its place. The poem stands in plain sight; the code lives only in the key-holder's mind.
