黄道十二宫杀手密码（Zodiac Killer Cipher）

> 密件册 · 1968–1974 年 · 美国加州旧金山湾区 · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：detective

说明

中文：1960-70 年代活跃于美国加州的连环杀手，自称「黄道十二宫」，向报社寄出多封加密信件自夸罪行；其中 408 密文被高中生夫妇破译，而更复杂的 340 密文直到 2020 年才由民间团队解开，其身份至今成谜。

English: A serial killer who terrorized the San Francisco Bay Area in the late 1960s and early 1970s, taunting newspapers with encrypted letters under the self-chosen name "Zodiac." The 408-character cipher was solved by a high-school couple; the harder 340 cipher fell only in 2020, to amateur codebreakers. His identity is still unknown.

全文 / 摘录

中文：1969 年 8 月 1 日，杀手把 408 密文一分为三寄给三家报社，要求头版刊载；密文破解后读出一段狂言：「我喜欢杀人，因为杀人实在太有趣了。」同年 11 月他又寄出 340 密文，署名处画着十字准星符号。此后还有一段 13 字符密码至今未破，被称为「我的名字是……」谜题。

English: On August 1, 1969, the killer split his 408-character cipher into three parts and mailed them to three newspapers, demanding front-page publication; it decoded to a boast: "I like killing people because it is so much fun." In November he mailed the 340 cipher signed with a crosshair symbol. A final 13-character message — the "My name is..." puzzle — also remains unsolved.
