说明

中文：戚继光《纪效新书》所载反切码：取两首诗词，「重唱诗」取二十声母、「合声诗」取四十字韵母，声韵两两交叉得八百音码，再配以金鼓旗号传递——将本土音韵学化作军中密码。以下为依原法重构的编码页。

English: The fanqie code in Qi Jiguang's Jixiao Xinshu: take two poems — twenty initials from one, forty finals from the other — cross twenty initials with forty finals to form eight hundred sound-codes, then signal them by gongs, drums and flags, turning phonology into an army cipher.
