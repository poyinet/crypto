凯撒战报（Caesar's Dispatch）

> 密件册 · 公元前 1 世纪 · 性质：据史料复原 / Reconstructed · 关联游戏：caesar

说明

中文：凯撒战报——高卢战争期间以移位密码写就的军令，机密内容字母移位成文，防止信使被截泄密。

English: Caesar's Dispatch — a Gallic War order written in a shifted alphabet, so that a captured courier could not betray the plan.

全文 / 摘录

中文：（战报体史料化节选）「军团报告前线平静。黎明时分，三支大队将开赴河渡口换防，粮草已备二十日。若敌军异动，我军先行。——盖乌斯·尤利乌斯·凯撒」

English: Veni, vidi, vici. — "I came, I saw, I conquered." (Caesar to the Senate, 47 BC)

Dispatch excerpt (reconstructed):

"The legions report all quiet along the front. Three cohorts march at dawn to relieve the garrison at the river ford; twenty days of rations are laid in. If the enemy moves, we move first. Written in the shifted cipher, lest this letter fall into Gallic hands. — C. Julius Caesar"
