克里普托斯（Kryptos）

> 密件册 · 1990 年 · 美国弗吉尼亚（CIA 总部） · 性质：真实史料 / Real record · 关联游戏：maker

说明

中文：矗立于 CIA 总部广场的铜制雕塑，镌刻 865 个字符的加密铭文，四段密文中三段已被破解，第四段至今无人解出，答案只存在于创作者脑中。

English: A copper sculpture in the plaza of CIA headquarters, bearing 865 characters of encrypted text in four sections. Three have been cracked; the fourth — K4 — remains unsolved, its answer known only to the artist.

全文 / 摘录

中文：第一段铭文破译后读到：「在微妙的光影之间，藏着幻象的精微之处」（Between subtle shading and the absence of light lies the nuance of iqlusion）。第三段结尾更抛出谜题：「CAN YOU SEE ANYTHING Q？」（「你能看到什么了吗？Q？」——化用卡特开启图坦卡蒙墓的记述），而最后的第四段 K4，至今仍让全球密码爱好者夜不能寐。

English: Decoded segments read: "Between subtle shading and the absence of light lies the nuance of iqlusion," and K3 ends with the riddle "Does it hide in the dark?" The unsolved fourth passage, K4, still keeps cryptographers around the world awake at night.
